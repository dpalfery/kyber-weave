# test skills

## What the testing skill covers

- Unit-test patterns.
- Integration-test patterns, including how a test obtains a real database.
- End-to-end and Playwright patterns.
- Mock-usage analysis: what may be substituted and what must stay real.
- Test maintainability: fixtures, builders and fakes.

## Hand-off

When ceding work to delivery, provide the test command, its filter expression and its output
format, so CI runs the command validated locally.
